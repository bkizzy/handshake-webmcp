# Mutual Assent AI demo video script

Target: 90–110 seconds. Hard maximum: 2:00.

Story: open on the payoff—a burst of bilateral negotiation, approval, celebration, and the executed agreement—then rewind to show how two independent agents reached it through WebMCP.

Voiceover lines are written to be read aloud. The screen should carry the proof; the narration supplies the story.

---

## Cold open: the payoff (0:00–0:15)

**Screen:** Music starts immediately. Rapid montage, alternating between the author and signer:

- Signer agent proposes the focused redlines.
- Author agent reviews and clears one.
- A counter appears on the signer's side.
- Remaining redlines resolve in quick succession.
- Both sides reach approval.
- Flash the “ready to sign” email.
- Show the author’s celebration/success state—the only celebration shot.
- Flash the “document executed” email after signing.
- Cut briefly to the executed PDF, with both party email addresses matted out, then scroll upward through the sealed agreement.
- Land on the two signature blocks or executed status.

Use quick, readable cuts rather than showing entire agent responses. Let the product state changes provide continuity between the two sides.

**On-screen title:** “Two companies. Two agents. One agreement.”

**VO:**
“Two independent AI agents just negotiated this NDA—each representing a different company, each protecting its own side. The humans kept control of the decisions that mattered, and signed the final agreement.”

## Rewind (0:15–0:19)

**Screen:** Scroll to the bottom of the executed agreement and push in on the verified seal/hash. Hold long enough for it to register, then rewind through the negotiation montage to the Mutual Assent AI start screen.

**VO:**
“They achieved mutual assent. And this is how it happened.”

---

## The sequence (0:19–1:18)

### Beat 1 — The author delegates (0:19–0:34)

**Screen:** Show the author and the Mutual Assent AI site together. The author gives the agent a concise business instruction. As the agent authenticates, briefly cut to the first OTP email, then return to the browser as the code is accepted. The agent creates the NDA, fills the parties and business terms, and sends it for review. Use the invitation-confirmed state as the end of the beat.

**VO:**
“Northstar’s author gives her agent the objective and its negotiating boundaries. The agent opens Mutual Assent AI, creates the NDA, fills the structured terms, and sends it to the other side.”

### Beat 2 — The signer’s agent takes over (0:34–0:49)

**Screen:** Brief email still: the real review invitation. Cut immediately to the signer’s independent agent and the agreement page. Show the signer’s concise instruction, followed by the agent inspecting the agreement and proposing only the required changes.

**VO:**
“Signal Forge receives the invitation. Its own agent opens the same agreement with a different mandate: narrow the purpose, protect pre-existing work, and limit ordinary confidentiality to three years.”

### Beat 3 — WebMCP negotiation (0:49–1:06)

**Screen:** Alternate between both live agreement views. As the first changes arrive, flash the redlines/action-required email, then cut directly into the corresponding redlines in the product. Show proposals, counters, reasoning, activity attribution, and the progress toward agreement. Include the signer agent’s short question to its human and the human’s brief answer. Then show the agents completing the remaining negotiation.

**VO:**
“WebMCP lets each agent work through the product’s own trusted tools. There is no shared agent and no hidden handoff of private context. Each side reads the same current agreement, acts within its authority, and leaves an attributed record. When a real business judgment is needed, the agent asks its human. Then the negotiation continues.”

### Beat 4 — Humans sign (1:06–1:18)

**Screen:** Both sides approved. Flash the ready-to-sign email, then show the human signing moments. After the second signature, flash the document-executed email and cut directly to the sealed agreement. Use only the author’s celebration/success shot; do not imply that a signer celebration shot exists.

**VO:**
“The agents negotiate and approve the language. They never sign. Each human reviews and signs for their own company, and the agreement becomes sealed and read-only.”

---

## Proof and close (1:18–1:35)

**Screen:** Scroll the clean executed NDA, then show the Certificate of Negotiation and verified seal. End on the Mutual Assent AI name and mutualassent.com.

**VO:**
“Traditional e-signing proves what was signed. Mutual Assent AI also preserves how every term changed, who proposed it, and who approved it. WebMCP makes the website the agent interface—so independent agents can work together without a custom integration between them.”

**End card:**

“Mutual Assent AI”

“Agents negotiate. Humans decide and sign.”

“mutualassent.com”

Small disclosure: “AI-generated narration.”

## Agent-chat visibility rules

Only show dialogue that belongs naturally to the agreement workflow. Crop, reframe, blur, or replace any shot that exposes production coordination.

Good on-camera content:

- The human’s concise business objective and negotiating boundaries.
- The agent’s short confirmation of what it created, reviewed, or proposed.
- Genuine tool activity tied to the visible agreement.
- The one business escalation and the human’s answer.
- “All redlines are resolved” and “ready for human signature” status messages.

Never show:

- Shooting instructions, retake requests, or discussion of screen recording.
- Requests to fullscreen, switch displays, pause, or wait for a cue.
- Test/debug messages, failed setup attempts, or conversation about which URL to use.
- Passwords, full one-time codes, private links, or unrelated email content.
- Long agent responses that cannot be read comfortably before the cut.

Preferred visible author instruction:

> Create a mutual NDA with Signal Forge for evaluating a warehouse-automation integration. You may accept a two-to-four-year confidentiality term and narrow the purpose. Ask me before agreeing to any model-training use. Do not sign.

Preferred visible signer instruction:

> Review this NDA for Signal Forge. Protect our pre-existing orchestration work, narrow the purpose to this evaluation, and keep ordinary confidentiality to three years. Ask me if you need a business decision. Do not sign.

Preferred escalation:

> **Agent:** Should I identify the pre-existing library by name, or describe it by category?
>
> **Human:** Use the broader category.

## Editing and audio notes

- Treat the opening as a cinematic teaser: 1–2 second shots, strong music pulse, and clean product-state match cuts.
- Make the celebration brief and credible; the executed agreement is the real payoff.
- Use email stills as fast connective tissue, not the subject of the demo.
- Protect four email beats: first OTP, redlines/action required, ready to sign, and document executed. Other emails are optional.
- Present each email as a short notification-style insert or tight crop, then match-cut to the state it refers to in Mutual Assent AI.
- Use only the author’s celebration footage.
- Keep the browser and product dominant. When agent chat is necessary, use a clean split view and crop tightly to the relevant exchange.
- Normalize the footage to 1920×1080 at 30 fps before the final edit.
- Deliver narration captions as separate SRT/VTT tracks, limited to one or two short lines at a time.
- Lower the music under narration and let it rise during the cold-open montage, rewind, and end card.
- Record final narration only after picture lock.
